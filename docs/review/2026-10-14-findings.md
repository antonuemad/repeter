Each was confirmed by an independent verifier who tried to refute it. All were then fixed by owners with disjoint files, integrated, and re-verified. `Reverify` is the verdict of a second skeptic after the fixes (`pending` = that group's re-verification had not finished when this file was written; check the workflow result or re-run it).
