1. Who we are

Repeater ("Repeater", "we", "us") is a service operated by Magic Lap. Repeater helps creators and businesses re-publish their own short videos to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels on a schedule they control. The service is available at {{ base_url }}.

Questions about this policy or your data: {{ contact_email }}.

2. Information we collect

  • Your dashboard account: your email address, a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself), your language preference and the time of your last sign-in.
  • Connected accounts: when you connect TikTok, YouTube, Google Drive, Facebook or Instagram, we store the account identifiers and public profile details the platform returns (account, channel or Page ID, display name, username, profile picture link), the permissions you granted, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens. Tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are stored and are never written to logs.
  • Videos and captions you import: the video files you upload or that Repeater imports from the sources you configure, their titles, captions, original publish dates and links, technical details (duration, resolution, file size), thumbnails, and fingerprints (SHA-256 and perceptual hashes) used only to avoid publishing the same video twice.
  • Publishing records: your rotation rules and post settings, the schedule, the status of each post, and the post IDs and links returned by the platforms.
  • Activity logs: a log of events in your workspace (imports, publishes, errors, connections, sign-ins) and which user performed them.
  • Your consent: the time and the version of the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy that you accepted when you first signed in.
  • Technical data: our servers record request details such as IP address and browser user agent for security, abuse prevention (for example sign-in rate limiting) and to detect leaks of temporary video links.
  • Cookies: one essential session cookie (to keep you signed in and protect forms) and one cookie that remembers your language. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

3. Why we use it

We use this information only to provide the features you ask for: importing videos from the sources you choose, preparing them for each platform, publishing them to the accounts you connected according to your schedule and settings, showing you their status, and alerting you about problems. We also use it to keep the service secure. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build profiles about you, and we do not use your content or platform data to train machine-learning models.

4. When we share it

  • With the platforms you choose: when Repeater publishes, it sends the video, title, caption and post settings to the platform account you selected. Instagram, Facebook and TikTok may download the video from a temporary signed link that expires within hours.
  • With service providers that host our servers and storage on our behalf, under confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Alerts to the people who operate the service (Telegram): when something needs attention (a post could not be published, an account must be reconnected, an import failed), Repeater sends a message to a private Telegram chat of the Magic Lap team that operates the service. An alert can contain the title of the video, the name of the connected account (for example a YouTube channel title) and the error text returned by the platform. Telegram processes these messages under its own privacy policy. We read alerts only to operate the service and to investigate failures and abuse.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the service.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data or your platform data.

5. YouTube API Services

Repeater uses YouTube API Services. When you connect a YouTube channel, Repeater accesses your channel ID, channel title, custom URL and uploads playlist, reads the list of your channel's uploads (only if you choose the channel as a source, to detect your new videos), and uploads the videos you scheduled with the title, description, privacy status and other settings you chose. By using the YouTube features of Repeater you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke Repeater's access to your Google and YouTube data at any time from the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (also reachable at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions), or by disconnecting the channel in the Repeater dashboard. While a YouTube channel is connected, the YouTube API data we store (the channel title and picture, and the YouTube video IDs and links recorded on posts and in the activity log) is refreshed or removed at least every {{ platform_retention_days }} days.

In particular: we use Google user data (YouTube and Google Drive) only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above; we do not transfer it to others except (a) to provide or improve the user-facing features described above, with your consent, (b) for security purposes (for example, investigating abuse), (c) to comply with applicable law, or (d) as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, after we have obtained your explicit prior consent; we do not use or transfer it for advertising; and no person at Magic Lap reads it unless you give us permission for a specific support request, it is needed for security purposes (such as investigating abuse or a failure reported by an alert, see section 4), or the law requires it. Google Drive access uses a read-only permission that technically covers all your Drive files. 7. TikTok

When you connect TikTok, we receive your TikTok account identifier (open_id), display name, profile picture and username, and tokens that allow Repeater to publish on your behalf. Each time you open your TikTok post settings, Repeater asks TikTok for your current creator settings (nickname, allowed privacy options, whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, and the maximum video length). Repeater publishes only with the privacy level, interaction settings, content disclosure and Music Usage Confirmation that you approved in those settings, and you can turn TikTok publishing off at any time. Disconnecting TikTok in the dashboard deletes the stored tokens and revokes Repeater's access at TikTok, unless the same TikTok user is still connected in another Repeater workspace (revoking removes the app from your TikTok permissions altogether, so in that case only this workspace's tokens are deleted). You can also remove Repeater from the app permissions in your TikTok account settings.

8. Facebook and Instagram (Meta)

With direct Instagram Login, we receive your professional account ID, app-scoped user ID, username, account type and profile picture, granted permissions and an encrypted Instagram user access token. A Facebook Page is not required. We use this access for your own media library and chosen content plan; we do not request private messages or comments. During the invitation-only test, all actual publishing remains locked.

For direct login, remove Repeater in Instagram's Apps and websites settings to revoke access across all workspaces. Disconnecting in Repeater deletes this workspace's token and stops its work; it does not revoke other workspaces' grants. Instagram deauthorization and data-deletion callbacks are authenticated using the Instagram app secret and processed separately from Facebook callbacks.

When you connect with Facebook, we receive your app-scoped Facebook user ID, the Facebook Pages you chose and their access tokens, and the Instagram professional accounts linked to those Pages (ID and username). We use them to list and download your own videos when you choose a Page or Instagram account as a source, and to publish Reels to the accounts you selected.

You can remove Repeater from your Facebook account settings (Apps and Websites, or Business Integrations for Pages). When you do, Meta notifies us and we mark those accounts as disconnected and stop all publishing for them. If you ask Meta to delete your data, Meta sends us a deletion request. The deletion starts at once and is completed within 24 hours: we delete the Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts connected through that Facebook login, the videos imported from them and their files, the posts and publishing history of those accounts, and every activity-log entry that names them. We give you a confirmation code and a status page at {{ base_url }}/meta/deletion-status/<code>.

9. Optional unofficial import (TikTok and YouTube)

TikTok and YouTube do not offer an official way to download your own videos. Repeater offers an optional import from a public TikTok or YouTube profile using the open-source tool yt-dlp. It is off by default; you must switch it on for each source and confirm that the profile and its content are yours and that you accept the risk. This import is not part of our TikTok or Google API integrations and never uses the tokens or the official API of any of your connected accounts: detecting and downloading the videos is done by yt-dlp alone. Downloading content this way may conflict with those platforms' terms, and you are responsible for using it. The safer alternatives are uploading the original file in the dashboard or using a Google Drive folder.

Invitations and account recovery

During the invitation-only pilot, administrators share invitation and recovery links manually through a verified contact channel. Invitation links expire after 72 hours and recovery links after 30 minutes; each works once. We store only link digests, password hashes, invitation email addresses and the operator audit record. Recovery request email addresses are encrypted. Abuse prevention uses keyed digests of email addresses and IP addresses. Requesting recovery does not change your password; completing it ends previous sign-in sessions.

Expired invitation and reset records are scheduled for deletion after seven days, recovery requests after seven days from creation, and abuse prevention counters after one day. Audit events follow the activity-log retention below. We do not send these messages automatically during the pilot.

10. How long we keep data

  • Original files are deleted after {{ original_retention_days }} days (once the prepared copy exists). Originals of videos that failed to process are deleted after 7 days.
  • Prepared copies and thumbnails stay in your library until you delete the video (owners have a Delete button on every video), ask us to delete them, or close your workspace.
  • OAuth tokens are deleted immediately when you disconnect the account, and otherwise kept only until the platform revokes access.
  • Disconnected accounts: within 7 days of disconnecting an account we delete the account record (identifiers, display name, picture), its post history (platform post IDs and links) and the activity-log entries that name it. Videos imported from that account stay in your library until you delete them or ask us to delete your data. For retained Meta imports we keep the authorizing Facebook or Instagram app-scoped user ID only as a deletion lookup key, so a later Meta deletion request can still remove those videos; the key is deleted with them.
  • YouTube data: while a YouTube channel is connected, the YouTube API data we store (the channel title and picture, the video IDs and links on published, canceled, failed and needs-attention posts, and the same identifiers in the activity log) is refreshed or removed within {{ platform_retention_days }} days.
  • Meta data deletion requests are completed within 24 hours, as described in section 8.
  • Activity logs are deleted after 90 days. Publishing records are kept while your workspace exists and are deleted with it.
  • Backups: we keep database backups for disaster recovery: daily copies for 14 days on the server and up to 30 days off-site, and a few safety copies made before a software update or a restore for at most 30 days. Data you deleted, or that a retention rule above removed, can remain in those copies until they expire (within 30 days at the latest); they are used only to recover the service.
  • Server logs are rotated and kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

11. Deleting your data and your choices

  • Disconnect any account in the dashboard (Accounts page). Repeater deletes the tokens at once, revokes access where the platform allows it and stops all work for that account; the account's records are deleted within 7 days (section 10).
  • Delete a video yourself: owners can delete any video from its page in the library. Its files, post history and related activity entries are removed at once; posts already on the platforms are not touched.
  • Email us at {{ contact_email }} to access, correct or delete your data, or to close your workspace. We complete deletion requests within 7 days, and answer other requests within 30 days.
  • Meta: remove the app in Facebook, or use Facebook's data deletion request; we process it through our data deletion callback within 24 hours, as described above.
  • Google: revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

12. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM using keys stored separately from the database. Links to video files are signed and expire. Access to production systems is restricted to the people who operate the service, and tokens are never shown to anyone, including our staff.
